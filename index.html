How RoBalancer works

01

Model your progression

Enter starting cash, income, first upgrade cost, cost growth, income growth, and upgrade count. The tool creates a linear upgrade path.

02

Measure bottlenecks

It estimates how long each purchase takes and flags slow waits, steep cost growth, and upgrades with little income impact.

03

Test alternatives

Use the multiplier comparison to see how a different cost curve changes modeled progression time before changing your game.

04

Validate in playtests

These are math-based recommendations, not guaranteed retention predictions. Actual fun and player behavior need playtesting.

Current limitations

  • No automatic Roblox account or game connection.
  • No direct parsing or editing of Roblox Studio place files.
  • One linear upgrade path; rebirths, upgrade trees, boosts, offline earnings, and multiple currencies must be modeled separately or added in future versions.
  • Recommendations are based on your inputs and transparent rules of thumb.
